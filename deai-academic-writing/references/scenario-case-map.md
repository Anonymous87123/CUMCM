## Cross-scene reuse rule

When a child skill reuses a case from another scene:

1. Name the transferred invariant, such as object identity or source alignment.
2. Do not transfer the source scene's voice, section template, or acceptance
   criterion.
3. Replace the example with a same-scene case when drafting user-facing prose.
4. Keep the original source attribution and do not claim the case proves
   prevalence in the receiving scene.
