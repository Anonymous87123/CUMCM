## Model chain

`conditional color grouping -> reference-quality gate -> hot-deck or mode imputation -> 85%--105% valid-sum screen -> multiplicative replacement of rounded zeros -> closure to 100% -> Spearman/frequency/chi-square -> Aitchison geometric means -> paired full and SiO2-removed charts -> Dirichlet regression -> clr transform -> 47/20 decision tree -> PbO threshold -> PLS-DA/VIP confirmation -> WSS choice of k=3 -> K-means subclasses -> subclass PLS-DA/VIP -> ten-segment CV for PLS dimension -> unknown-sample tree/PLS agreement -> subclass PLS -> type-specific Pearson matrices -> upper-triangle deduplication -> paired Wilcoxon`

PCA, ordinary linear regression, and a separate residual test are not part of the implemented chain.

## Reported results

- Missing colors: sample 40 is dark green, 58 light blue by hot-deck references; 19 and 48 are filled light blue by the mode. Samples 15 and 17 fail the 85%--105% composition-sum gate.
- Chi-square p-values for weathering versus pattern, glass type, and color are 0.085, 0.024, and 0.325; the paper retains only the type association.
- The decision tree uses `PbO=1.5`, with 47 training and 20 test samples, and reports 100% test accuracy.
- Type PLS-DA VIP values above 1 are PbO 2.4614, K2O 1.8000, BaO 1.3472, and SrO 1.1113.
- Both glass types use k=3 subclasses. Lead-barium clusters separate more clearly; high-potassium glass is explicitly described as only roughly divisible into three groups.
- Three PLS components are chosen using both CV RMSEP and cumulative explained variance. Unknown samples A1/A6/A7 are high-potassium; A2/A3/A4/A5/A8 are lead-barium. The tree and PLS classifications agree.
- The paired Wilcoxon test on corresponding upper-triangle correlations gives `p=0.905`; the paper does not reject equal correlation relationships.

## Human style rules extracted

1. For one missing field, write the matching conditions, candidate IDs, and reference-quality difference before choosing hot-deck or mode.
2. Distinguish blank detections from reported zeros, explain why neither is an ordinary zero for log-ratio analysis, then give multiplicative replacement and closure.
3. When one component masks the rest, retain the complete chart, add a chart excluding the dominant component, and combine both before drawing a conclusion.
4. Let a simple decision rule provide interpretability, then use a multivariate PLS-DA/VIP model as confirmation rather than as a decorative second method.
5. State explicitly that unknown subclass labels mean no response variable and therefore an unsupervised task.
6. Preserve unequal cluster clarity: one category may separate clearly while another is only roughly grouped.
7. Choose latent dimension with cross-validated error and cumulative explained variance together.
8. Derive the test from data structure: symmetric matrices justify upper-triangle deduplication; corresponding component pairs justify a paired test.
