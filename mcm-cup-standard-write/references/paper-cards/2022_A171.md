## Model chain

`equilibrium -> piecewise restoring force -> coupled heave ODE -> Laplace for constant damping / RK4 for power-law damping -> RLC force-electric analogy -> trapezoidal stable-window optimization -> center of mass and inertia -> heave-pitch ODE -> two-damper optimization -> analytic-vs-RK4 error check`

## Reported results

- Equilibrium spring compression is about `0.2980 m`, and draft about `2.8 m`.
- Constant damping: impedance matching gives `k3≈37193.81 N s/m`, `P≈229.334 W`.
- Power-law damping: `k3=beta|v1-v2|^alpha`, `beta in [0,100000]`, `alpha in [0,1]`; RK4 plus trapezoidal integration gives `P≈228.89 W`, `beta=100000`, `alpha≈0.3975`.
- Heave-pitch coupling uses a small-angle assumption and an eight-state `ode45` model.
- Two-damper optimization reports `P≈327.96 W`, `k3≈62140`, `L3≈95541` in the stated bounds; direct and rotational damping contribute `317.75 W` and `10.21 W`.
- Validation uses the constant-damping analytic solution as the RK4 reference and evaluates power on the final ten stable periods.

## Human style rules extracted

1. Write equilibrium position and geometric applicability before the dynamic equation.
2. Let coefficient type authorize the method: constant -> Laplace; velocity-dependent -> RK4.
3. Define instantaneous power, averaging window, decisions and constraints before optimization.
4. Use prior-question results to authorize reuse: `沿用第一问建立的模型`, `参照问题二建立最优化模型`.
5. Keep identical time points and state-variable order in result tables so cases can be read side by side.
6. Turn validation into a judgment: identify the transient, quantify the stable-stage error, and state whether the approximation is acceptable.

## Boundaries

These observations support A-type mechanical-model writing only under the stated assumptions. They do not license universal use of Laplace transform, RK4, `ode45`, or global grid search. Intermediate expressions not raster-checked must not be copied as exact equations.
