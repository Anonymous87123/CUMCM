Genetic algorithms and a separate residual test are not part of the implemented chain. The earlier automatic hits came from the coordinate symbol `G_a` and cylinder-intersection text.

## Reported results

- Q1 annual mean output is `36.994507 MW`; optical, cosine, shadow/blocking, and truncation efficiencies are `0.605150`, `0.756465`, `0.925464`, and `0.980774`; unit-area output is `0.588897 kW/m^2`.
- Q2 uses tower `(0,-79)`, `5.5 m × 5.5 m` mirrors at `2.75 m`, 3385 mirrors, and `102396.3 m^2`; annual output is `60.118891 MW`, with `0.587120 kW/m^2` unit-area output.
- Q3 uses tower `(0,-79)`, `6.4 m × 6.4 m` mirrors, seven heights from `3.2 m` to `3.8 m`, 2846 mirrors, and `116572.2 m^2`; annual output is `61.859281 MW`, with `0.530652 kW/m^2` unit-area output.
- The mixed layout beats either single layout strategy on the same four annual-mean optical metrics.
- Collector-height and diameter perturbations yield optical-efficiency changes of `-13.9%--0.51%` and `-7.7%--2.3%`. For tower heights from `50 m` to `110 m`, cosine efficiency is most sensitive and rises by more than `11.2%`.

## Human-writing actions

2. Isolate the true difficulty: `quantities already supplied by the prompt -> remaining unknown efficiencies -> computational focus`.
3. Justify an engineering approximation through `exact-boundary burden -> numerical dominance -> central symmetry/statistical compensation -> center-based binary rule`.
4. Turn geometry into an executable decision through `A frame -> field frame -> B frame -> plane intersection -> inside/outside -> valid/invalid point`.
5. Close truncation by energy accounting: `full reflected energy -> invalid-point loss -> receiver hits -> efficiency ratio`.
6. Convert spatial evidence into design: `efficiency cloud -> high-efficiency direction -> mirror concentration -> opposite tower shift -> bounded siting variable`.
7. Explain search by its compute budget: `direct exhaustive cost -> binary/coarse localization -> initial interval -> smaller steps -> accuracy stop`.
8. Write sensitivity as `range and step -> change interval -> asymmetric direction -> saturation -> ranked components`, not as a generic robustness claim.
