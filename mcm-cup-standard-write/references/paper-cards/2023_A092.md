## Reported results

- Q1 optical, cosine, shadow/blocking, and truncation efficiencies are `0.6275/0.7599/0.9952/0.9342`; output is `38.295 MW`, with `0.6096 kW/m^2` unit-area output.
- Q2 uses tower `(0,-250)`, `5.55×5.55 m` mirrors at `5 m`, 2739 mirrors, and `84368.0475 m^2`; efficiencies are `0.7394/0.8817/0.9978/0.9568`, output is `60.373 MW`, and unit-area output is `0.7139 kW/m^2`.
- Q3 uses tower `(0,-250)`, 2315 mirrors, and `87633.4929 m^2`; efficiencies are `0.7818/0.9418/0.9968/0.9462`, output is `60.359 MW`, and unit-area output is `0.7551 kW/m^2`.
- Q2 improves optical efficiency, output, and unit-area output over Q1 by `17.86%/57.65%/17.11%`. Q3 improves optical and unit-area efficiency over Q2 by about `5.8%/5.77%`, while total output is essentially unchanged.

## Evidence boundary
