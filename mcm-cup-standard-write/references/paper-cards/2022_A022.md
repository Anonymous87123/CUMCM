## Model chain

`静水顶端高度边界 -> 垂荡二阶耦合 ODE -> ode45 -> 稳态窗口 trapz -> 复振幅解析解/数值解对照 -> 粗扫--局部细化幂次阻尼 -> 质心/惯量 -> 平动非惯性系纵摇 -> 双阻尼遍历 -> 频谱、小量与刚度边界检验`。

1. Heave: fixed and power-law damping equations for buoy/oscillator; `ode45`; tables at 10/20/40/60/100 s.
2. Constant damping: complex-amplitude response gives `eta_i=37193.8119 N s/m`, `P=229.3339 W`; `trapz` over `200--400 s` gives `229.497276437679 W` near `3.72e4 N s/m`.
3. Power-law damping: no convenient analytic solution; 2D sweep `m in [0,100000]`, `alpha in [0,1]`, then local refinement. `P=230.12739 W`, `alpha=0.370`, `m=9.75e4`.
4. Heave/pitch: centroid and inertia from cylinder/cone/cover decomposition; small relative heave triggers whole-body pitch and a translational non-inertial frame.
5. Two damping coefficients: global sweep, monotonic rotation-damping observation, local refinement. `Pmax=322.128795508752 W`, `eta_i=5.93e4`, `eta_j=100000`.

## Verification boundary

- `h_0=0.99999 m`; all four-question buoy displacements remain below `1.00 m`.
- Frequency analysis gives about `1.399 rad/s`, close to excitation frequency.
- Relative heave ratio `[0.09,0.10]`; pitch angles about `6.88 deg` and `7.73 deg`.
- Excitation and stiffness scans support the small-quantity assumptions in the stated range; torsional stiffness below a threshold causes model failure.
