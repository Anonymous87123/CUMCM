# 2023 A0165: mechanism-based heliostat-field design

## Evidence scope

- A problem, 45 pages; p.1--45 were manually read, including all three annual tables and MATLAB appendices.
- Evidence IDs: `EV-20260730-A0165-01`, `EV-20260730-A0165-STYLE-01`.
- Full-paper RapidOCR was used. Pages 18 and 21 were rerendered at 220 DPI and re-OCRed with Tesseract `chi_sim+eng`; six raster pages were checked through JPEG proxies below 450 KB.

## Model chain

`point-in-rectangle via two triangles -> solar angles -> incident/reflected directions and mirror normal -> mirror grid -> six-nearest-neighbor line/plane intersections -> incident shadow and reflected blocking -> conical-spot receiver interception -> five optical efficiencies -> DNI and field output -> 60 annual sample times -> spatial map showing northern efficiency advantage -> southward tower shift -> concentric-ring layout -> 2×2 fast evaluation kernel -> one-variable-at-a-time reduction -> unimodal D and W curves -> ternary search -> common-size design -> freeze W/D/layout -> Monte Carlo ring-height search -> sampling-time structural validation`

GA, PSO, SA, and a separate residual test are not implemented. Monte Carlo is used only for the per-ring installation-height search in Q3.

## Reported results

- Q1 optical, cosine, shadow/blocking, and truncation efficiencies are `0.4902`, `0.7565`, `0.8955`, and `0.8078`; annual output is `24.04 MW`, with `0.3827 kW/m^2` unit-area output.
- Q2 uses tower `(0,-237.0240)`, `5.4670 m × 5.4670 m` mirrors at `6 m`, 3249 mirrors, and `97106.40 m^2`; the four efficiencies are `0.5787/0.8614/0.8546/0.8811`, output is `43.73 MW`, and unit-area output is `0.4505 kW/m^2`.
- Q3 freezes Q2's `W`, `D`, and layout and searches heights by ring. The reported aggregate uses tower `(0,-237.0240)`, 3249 mirrors, and `97106.40 m^2`; the four efficiencies are `0.5856/0.8625/0.8642/0.8804`, output is `44.25 MW`, and unit-area output is `0.4556 kW/m^2`.
- The reported unimodal maxima occur at southward shift `D=237.0240 m` and mirror width `W=5.4670 m`.
- A `2×2` grid evaluation differs from the fuller Q1 calculation by less than `2%` according to the paper.
- Recomputing at `8:00, 8:30, 9:00, 9:30, 10:00` produces spatial-peak movement consistent with apparent solar motion.

## Evidence boundary

All numerical values are paper-reported and raster-checked; MATLAB code was read for interface verification but not executed. Per-ring Q3 heights are not fully printed in the paper and must not be invented. Ternary-search and Monte Carlo outputs are not a joint continuous global-optimum certificate. The paper has no separate parameter-sensitivity section; the under-2% grid comparison is only a numerical-resolution check.
